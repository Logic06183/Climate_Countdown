\begin{abstract}
The 2025 African Countdown on Health and Climate Change presents a comprehensive analysis of climate change impacts on health across Africa. This report, building on the global Lancet Countdown framework, provides region-specific insights and evidence-based recommendations for strengthening health system resilience. Drawing on data from multiple sectors and institutions, we highlight critical challenges and opportunities for action in the face of accelerating climate threats.
\end{abstract}
