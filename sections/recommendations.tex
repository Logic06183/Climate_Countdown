\section{Recommendations and Adaptation Strategies}
\label{sec:recommendations}

\subsection{Health System Resilience}
Based on recent research from sub-Saharan Africa (2024):

\begin{recommendation}
\textbf{Healthcare Infrastructure Adaptation:}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Implement climate-resilient design standards for health facilities
    \item Develop backup power and water systems
    \item Create mobile health units for emergency response
    \item Establish telemedicine capabilities for remote areas
    \item Build capacity for cold chain management
\end{enumerate}
\end{recommendation}

\subsection{Disease Surveillance and Prevention}
WHO African Region priorities for 2025:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Early Warning Systems:}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Integration of climate and health data
        \item Real-time disease outbreak monitoring
        \item Vector surveillance networks
        \item Environmental health tracking
    \end{itemize}
    
    \item \textbf{Prevention Strategies:}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Vector control programs
        \item Water quality monitoring
        \item Air quality management
        \item Heat-health action plans
    \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}
