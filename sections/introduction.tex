\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

\subsection{Background}
Climate change represents one of the most significant threats to human health and wellbeing in the 21st century, with particularly severe implications for Africa \citep{lancet2024}. The continent, despite contributing less than 4\% of global greenhouse gas emissions, faces disproportionate impacts on its health systems, economic development, and social stability \citep{africa2024}. The 2025 African Countdown on Health and Climate Change builds upon the global Lancet Countdown framework to provide a region-specific analysis of these challenges and opportunities.

\subsection{Current Context}
Recent data indicates that 2024 marked another year of record-breaking temperatures across Africa, with significant implications for public health \citep{who2024}. Key concerns include:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Rising Temperatures:} Increased frequency and intensity of heat waves, affecting vulnerable populations
    \item \textbf{Changing Disease Patterns:} Expanded ranges of vector-borne diseases and emerging health threats
    \item \textbf{Food Security:} Climate impacts on agricultural productivity and nutrition
    \item \textbf{Water Stress:} Growing water scarcity affecting health and sanitation
    \item \textbf{Health System Resilience:} Challenges in adapting healthcare infrastructure
\end{itemize}

\subsection{The African Countdown Initiative}
This report represents a collaborative effort between African institutions, international organizations, and health experts to:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Track progress on health and climate change in Africa
    \item Identify emerging threats and opportunities
    \item Support evidence-based policy making
    \item Promote regional cooperation and knowledge sharing
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Framework and Methodology}
Our analysis builds on the WHO African Region Framework 2024-2033 \citep{who2024}, incorporating:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Climate change exposure indicators
    \item Health impact assessments
    \item Adaptation and resilience metrics
    \item Policy response tracking
    \item Economic implications analysis
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Report Structure}
This report is organized into key sections:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Climate Impacts:} Analysis of current and projected climate effects
    \item \textbf{Health Implications:} Direct and indirect health impacts
    \item \textbf{Economic Implications:} Costs and investment requirements
    \item \textbf{Policy Response:} Current policies and implementation progress
    \item \textbf{Regional Case Studies:} Specific examples from across Africa
    \item \textbf{Recommendations:} Evidence-based policy guidance
\end{itemize}

\begin{keymessage}
\textbf{Key Context Points:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Africa faces disproportionate climate impacts despite minimal emissions
    \item Health systems require urgent adaptation and strengthening
    \item Regional cooperation is essential for effective response
    \item Evidence-based policy making needs strengthening
\end{itemize}
\end{keymessage}

\subsection{Scope and Limitations}
While comprehensive, this report acknowledges certain limitations:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Data availability varies across regions
    \item Some health impacts remain difficult to quantify
    \item Long-term projections carry inherent uncertainties
    \item Resource constraints affect monitoring capacity
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Call to Action}
The evidence presented in this report underscores the urgent need for:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Accelerated climate action
    \item Strengthened health systems
    \item Enhanced regional cooperation
    \item Increased climate finance
    \item Improved monitoring and evaluation
\end{itemize}

This introduction sets the stage for a detailed examination of how climate change affects health across Africa, and what can be done to build resilience and protect public health in the face of this growing challenge \citep{unep2024}.
