\section*{Executive Summary}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Executive Summary}

The 2025 African Countdown report presents the most up-to-date scientific evidence on the intersections of climate change and health across the African continent. This report tracks 43 indicators across five key domains, providing a comprehensive assessment of climate change impacts on health, adaptation efforts, and mitigation strategies specific to the African context.

\begin{keymessage}
    \textbf{Key Message 1: Accelerating Climate Impacts}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Temperature increases across Africa continue to outpace global averages
        \item Extreme weather events have increased in frequency and intensity
        \item Vector-borne disease patterns are shifting, with expanded geographical ranges
    \end{itemize}
\end{keymessage}

\begin{keymessage}
    \textbf{Key Message 2: Health System Vulnerability}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item African health systems face critical capacity constraints
        \item Climate-sensitive disease surveillance remains inadequate
        \item Infrastructure gaps limit adaptive capacity
    \end{itemize}
\end{keymessage}

\begin{keymessage}
    \textbf{Key Message 3: Adaptation and Resilience}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Growing recognition of climate-health linkages in policy
        \item Emerging innovative adaptation strategies
        \item Increased investment in climate-resilient healthcare
    \end{itemize}
\end{keymessage}

\begin{keymessage}
    \textbf{Key Message 4: Mitigation and Co-benefits}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Renewable energy adoption accelerating
        \item Air quality improvements from clean energy transition
        \item Economic benefits from sustainable development
    \end{itemize}
\end{keymessage}

\begin{recommendation}
    \textbf{Priority Actions for 2025-2030}
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item Strengthen health system resilience through targeted infrastructure investments
        \item Enhance disease surveillance and early warning systems
        \item Accelerate clean energy transition while maximizing health co-benefits
        \item Scale up climate finance for health adaptation
        \item Foster regional cooperation for climate-health research and response
    \end{enumerate}
\end{recommendation}
